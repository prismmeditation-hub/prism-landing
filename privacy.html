01
Who We Are
PRISM is a meditation and journaling app operated by Edward Willey (prismmeditation@gmail.com). References to "we," "us," or "our" refer to Edward Willey as the operator of PRISM. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.
02
What Data We Collect
We collect only what is necessary to provide the app experience:
  • Your first name — entered during onboarding
  • Your gender selection — used only to choose the pronouns in your generated insights. Specifying a gender is optional; if you choose not to, none is sent to the AI (see Section 6 for how we suggest a default)
  • Voice recordings — recorded during your practice sessions, intention, and completion reflections
  • Transcripts — text generated from your voice recordings
  • Content we generate for you — insights, letters, and artwork created from your reflections
  • App usage data — which days you have completed, your settings, and your commitment tracking
  • Account identifiers — an anonymous account ID is created for you automatically. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we also receive the identifier Apple provides (and a relay email address only if you choose to share one)
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your card details
  • Diagnostic data — crash and error reports, which contain technical metadata only and never your recordings, transcripts, or insights
We do not collect your precise location, and we do not sell your personal data.
03
How We Use Your Data
Your data is used to provide and improve your personal PRISM experience:
  • To transcribe your recordings and generate personalized AI insights, letters, and artwork
  • To track your progress through your challenge and display your journey
  • To choose the pronouns used in your generated insights
  • To provide and manage your subscription
  • To diagnose crashes and keep the app working
04
Voice Recordings and AI Processing
Short recordings are transcribed privately on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Longer recordings (roughly over 60 seconds) are transcribed securely by OpenAI's transcription service.

Your transcripts are then sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your insights and letters, and to compose the prompts used to create your artwork. Those image prompts are sent to OpenAI to generate the artwork.

All of these AI requests are routed through our secure server (api.yourprism.app), a Cloudflare Worker that verifies your identity and keeps our provider keys private. Anthropic and OpenAI process your data solely to generate your result. They do not use it to train their models, and they retain it only briefly for security purposes under their own API data-retention policies. You can review these at anthropic.com/privacy and openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
05
Where Your Data Is Stored
Your challenges, sessions, transcripts, insights, and settings are stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore). Your recordings and generated images are stored in cloud object storage (Cloudflare R2) and also cached on your device for quick access. Your data is linked to your anonymous account identifier.

Because your data is stored in the cloud, deleting the app does not delete your data. To remove your data, see Section 9 (Data Retention and Deletion).

Your Rights (UK and EU Users)
If you are located in the UK or European Union, you have the following rights under UK GDPR and EU GDPR:
  • Right of access — you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you may request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you may request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent — contact us and we will erase your data
  • Right to lodge a complaint — UK users may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). EU users may contact their local data protection authority.
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at prismmeditation@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. To make a request, contact us at prismmeditation@gmail.com.
09
Data Retention and Deletion
Your data stays stored until you delete it. It is not removed automatically — it remains even if your subscription ends or you stop using the app. You can delete all of your data at any time:
  • In the app, if you have signed in with Apple, use the account-deletion option to erase your account and data, or
  • Email us at prismmeditation@gmail.com and we will delete your data.
Deleting the app alone does not erase your cloud data. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database and storage. Data sent to our AI providers is deleted on their systems under their own retention policies. We will respond to deletion requests within 30 days.
10
Data Security
Your data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Our AI provider keys are held on our server and never ship inside the app. We rely on reputable providers (Google, Cloudflare, Apple) to store your data securely. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's Privacy
PRISM is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
